# Toy Story 2 (PS1) — PopTracker Pack

**Version 1.0.0 — Initial Release**
_June 2026_

The first release of the PopTracker pack for the **Toy Story 2 (PS1)** Archipelago
randomizer. It connects directly to your Archipelago server and auto-tracks your
progress as you play — no manual clicking required.

---

## Requirements

- **PopTracker** 0.18.0 or newer (built/tested against 0.32.0)
- A Toy Story 2 Archipelago game in progress (apworld **1.1.0+** recommended)
- An active connection to your Archipelago server

## What it does

- **Live auto-tracking.** Connects to your AP slot and lights up checks as you
  collect them — over 1,000 location mappings across the whole game.
- **Maps for the whole game.** Dedicated maps for all ten main levels, a Bosses
  map, and a **Level Select** hub that mirrors the in-game map screen.
- **Coinsanity coins hub.** The Level Select screen includes a per-level coin
  overview so you can see your coin progress for every level at a glance, with
  each level's own labeled coin icon. In-level coin checks use those same
  per-level icons for consistency.
- **Full item panel (106 entries).** Tracks everything you receive:
  - **Moves** — Double Jump, Stomp, Spin, Ledge Grab, Pole Climb, Pole Vault,
    Push, Rope Sliding, Visor, Laser
  - **Per-level gadgets** — Hover Boots, Rocket Boots, Cosmic Shield, Disc
    Launcher (tracked per level)
  - **Story & sanity items** — Batteries, Lives, Green Lasers, Hint Blocks,
    Pizza Planet Tokens, Final Showdown Tickets, Potato Head, Rex
  - **Missing toys & parts** — Sheep, Soldier, Worker Tike, Duck, Chick, Alien,
    Mouse, Passenger Tike, Critter, Luggage, plus Mr. Potato Head's Missing
    Arm / Ear / Eye / Foot / Mouth
- **Universal Tracker support.** Works alongside Universal Tracker for in-logic
  highlighting.

## How to use

1. Open PopTracker and load the **Toy Story 2** pack (Standard variant).
2. Start your game and connect the Archipelago client to your server.
3. The tracker will connect automatically and begin updating. Use the Level
   Select hub and the per-level maps to follow your remaining checks.

## Notes

- The pack reflects your sanity settings (Coinsanity, Lifesanity, Batterysanity,
  Green Laser Sanity, Hint Block Sanity, Movesanity, Rexsanity), so only the
  checks relevant to your seed appear as trackable.
- Reconnecting mid-session re-syncs cleanly from the server.

## Feedback

Found a mismarked check or a map that doesn't match your seed? Please report it
with your seed's settings so it can be reproduced.
